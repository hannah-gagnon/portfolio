Presentation House Theatre

A bolder, more recognizable brand for North Vancouver’s creative hub, built around community.

Timeline
November – December 2025
My role
Brand System Designer Social Media Mockups
Tools
Figma FigJam
Team
Claire Mackie Misha Gholami Joy Nguyen

(01) Overview

Modern, but not trendy. Recognizable, and built to be reused.

Presentation House Theatre (PHT) is a creative hub offering North Vancouver a dynamic mix of theatre, music, dance and more.

The goal

A cohesive identity that appeals to a wider range of North Van locals.

(02) Process

“Where Ideas Play” didn’t say what PHT does. “Celebrate Community. Celebrate Art.” does.

The Problem

The previous branding didn’t reflect what PHT actually does day-to-day, or their focus on community and the arts.

Exploration

After pitching three directions, we landed on bright colours and a bold, reusable logo that works on light and dark backgrounds.

(03) Brand system

Lots of iteration later: colours, type and a logo that are, most importantly, REUSABLE!

What we did

We iterated way more than shown here, and settled on a system that feels fresh without chasing trends.

*

Look closely

We subtly worked the letters P, H and T into the primary logo.

(04) Mockups

Creating a system that could be used across social media, advertising, merch, & more.

Social & beyond

We extended the system into social templates so the voice, colour and logo read consistently across posts and stories.
